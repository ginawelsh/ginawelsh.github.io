Mitigating Gender Bias in English–Ukrainian Machine Translation.
Pavels Ivanovs*, Gina Welsh*, Irini Selenica* — equal contribution
Accepted at EAMT 2026 (proceedings forthcoming)
  • Evaluated two bias-mitigation methods across two pre-trained MT models (OPUS-MT, mBART) on a curated dataset of occupation-based sentences.
  • Findings: LLM-based correction gave the strongest mitigation; gender tagging produced mixed results.
  • The framework uses Ukrainian's overt gender morphology as a bias signal and generalises to other grammatically gendered languages.
