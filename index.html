Hello!

I'm Gina. I'm a Natural Language Processing Master's student at Uppsala University in Sweden. I was a linguistics student at the Australian National University before I moved to the other side of the world.

My research interests lie in the intersection of linguistics and computer science, the big question being: how do computational models process human languages? Not just for English - but for the thousands of languages throughout the world. I am very interested in the interpretability of tools such as Large Language Models (LLMs); that is, how they create the human-like output that they are now capable of. What do they encode, and where do they fall short?

If you are a Swedish speaker, I invite you to play my web game AI eller inte, to help me gather data for how well Swedish speakers can detect LLM-generated Swedish text!

I'm using this site to document my programming projects and my research. It is still a work in progress. Feel free to browse and enjoy. If you want to reach out and collaborate/talk more, drop me a line at gina.welsh.7005@student.uu.se.
