Hej! / Hello!

I'm Gina Welsh, a natural language processing (NLP) researcher and Language Technology student at Uppsala University, Sweden. I completed my undergraduate degree in linguistics at the Australian National University, where I worked on corpus tools for low-resource Australian languages Warlpiri and Light Warlpiri.

My research focuses on LLM interpretability, machine translation evaluation, and the representation of low-resource and morphologically complex languages in NLP. The central question driving my work: what do large language models actually encode, and where do they systematically fail?

I recently presented with my co-authors at EAMT 2026 on gender bias mitigation in English-Ukrainian neural machine translation. My Master's thesis investigates automatic detection of LLM-generated versus human-written Swedish text, contributing to the growing field of AI-generated text detection.

If you're a Swedish speaker, test yourself on AI eller inte, a survey game collecting data on how well people detect AI-generated Swedish text!

I'm based in Uppsala, Sweden, and open to research collaborations in computational linguistics, multilingual NLP, and AI evaluation. Get in touch at gina.welsh.7005@student.uu.se.
